# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We aim to provide a respectful, inclusive, and constructive community for everyone participating in EntraOps, regardless of experience level, identity, background, or perspective.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, professional, and constructive.
- Focus discussion on technical evidence, reproducible behavior, and the project goals.
- Give and receive feedback in good faith.
- Respect privacy. Do not post credentials, tokens, tenant data, personal data, or other sensitive information.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, publication of private information, or behavior that makes participation unsafe or hostile is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject content and may limit participation when this Code of Conduct is violated. For private conduct concerns, contact the maintainers at security@entraops.com with "Code of Conduct" in the subject. Do not use that address for general support requests; see [SUPPORT.md](./SUPPORT.md).

This Code of Conduct applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and other spaces representing the EntraOps community.
